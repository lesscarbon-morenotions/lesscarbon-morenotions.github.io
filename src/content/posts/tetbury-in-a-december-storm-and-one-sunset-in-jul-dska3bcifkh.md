Tetbury in a December storm, and one sunset in July sunset🌬️ 🌄

I think I started noticing the amount of environmental/craft mid life crisis books on the shelves with a central theme of seasons before I began to appreciate them myself. A lot of us have various levels of attachment with different seasons for good and bad, but it also provides a sort of sureness. Climate change is of course shifting our seasons- from early springs, worse storms, and milder wet winters. Changes in radiative forcing and a weakening thermohaline circulation translating to declining biodiversity and changing migration patterns. 

In a the most direct sense, emotional regulation and food security the seasons give us is diminishing. We all lose. Don't make a billionaire happy this festive season - look after the soil, shop independent and look out for each other (human and non-human) xx 🪱🎁🫂🎄
