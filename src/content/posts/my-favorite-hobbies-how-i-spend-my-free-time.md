In our fast-paced world, it's easy to get caught up in work, responsibilities, and the demands of everyday life. Amidst all that noise, hobbies are how I reclaim attention. Here are three that keep me grounded.

## Reading

Reading is not just a hobby for me — it's a refuge. With a book in my hand, I can explore different worlds, understand different perspectives, and rest my mind without a screen.

Beyond entertainment, reading is a tool for self-improvement. It enhances vocabulary, sharpens critical thinking, and keeps empathy well-watered. A stack of books by the bed is my favourite kind of luxury.

## Gardening

Gardening is a tangible interaction with Mother Nature herself. There is something deeply satisfying about planting a seed, tending it, and watching life push through soil.

Gardening offers more than a sense of accomplishment. It provides a direct connection to nature, which can be incredibly calming. It's also a quiet lesson in carbon and cycles: compost, pollinators, seasons — the whole soft science of a small plot.

## Cooking

Cooking is my third favourite hobby and a creative outlet that lets me experiment and innovate. I love the entire process: choosing ingredients, trying techniques, and sharing the result.

Cooking also gives me the opportunity to share something tangible with people I love. A well-prepared meal is hospitality, culture, and care on a plate — and a chance to waste less when we plan well.

## Conclusion

Hobbies don't have to be productive to be valuable. Reading, gardening, and cooking help me spend free time in a way that feels restorative rather than extractive. If you're looking for a place to start, pick one small practice and give it room to grow.
