Still obsessed with the (greater scented) liverwort I found in the grotto of a domesticated water garden ngl 🥺🌿

A month of reading (and listening!) to a number of nature connection texts (and a touch of frolicking in autumn's riot) has led to an aggregation of learnings and thoughts spewed into twenty slides ✨ a few of these articles I re-read when I deleted my 963 inactive webpages on my phone 🙊 so now my brain feels freer and I better understood them based on current readings 🤷‍♀️ (now I am averaging 50 active tabs total, which is a significant improvement and my phone bestowed me with 2 GB of data)

We exist in nature's network, despite centuries of dualism trying to reverse this ideology. Although it sometimes feels like we in the West has all but severed our connection, I still believe deep down we're trying to reconnect: from irregular contact with the abundance of goods from the earth we chase consumer goods instead for the same emotions and problem-solving processes; Instagram could be perceived to be about selfhood, but do we actually want it to be for community and finding where we belong?
