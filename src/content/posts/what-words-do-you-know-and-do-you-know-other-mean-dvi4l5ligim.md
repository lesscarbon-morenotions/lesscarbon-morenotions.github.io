What words do you know? And do you know other meanings? 💚

I love these ones in particular, because they capture much of what we celebrate in connecting to nature and moving through the world mindfully and sustainably 🌟🍄🌸 They capture sensations, kinship, and states of being rather than nouns 🍃☀️. Tim Robinson said on the terrain of west Ireland "the landscape speaks Irish". After all, to learn about a place and living beings inside it, as with a few of these words, we can start to see how local language encompasses the feelings, traditions, and values of living with the land and each other 🍂🪨
