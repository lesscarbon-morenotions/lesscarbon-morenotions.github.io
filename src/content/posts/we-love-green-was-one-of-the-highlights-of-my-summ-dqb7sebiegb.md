We Love Green was one of the highlights of my summer, from getting to see Rachel after too many months, and then to seeing Charli herself for the first time 💚🎤

The festival was actually really cool in the way it promoted pro-enviro behaviour clearly, not just from veggie meals from an array of cuisines 🥙and climate talks🪜, but through renewable power☀️, composting urinals (an ✨experience✨), banning plastic water bottles🍶 and drones. It also did a deposit return scheme on reusable crockery, which was such a breath of fresh air. I feel like I've been to a good few events who low-key greenwash with "compostable" plates and bamboo cutlery, which are still waste at the end of the day.🎋

I can't speak for everyone, but both me and Rachel had a no-fly hol which drove our emissions down, with using Eurostar🚆, trains🚃, and bus/ferry🚌🛳️. Apparently We Love Green have calculated their Scope 3 emissions, but I'd be interested to see whether it accounted for international travel beyond Paris. There was also a "customise your own brat t-shirt" which is cute and all but I am not entirely sure if they were sourced ethically. Rachel sewed her dress and my fit was all second-hand so we slayed there at least 🤷🏼‍♀️

Critics have also blasted the festival for its noise pollution for bird, bat and insects in Bois de Vincennes 🐦🦇🪱. I can concur it was quite noisy, but any space festival go-ers went in was strictly non-vegetated other than the grass from an other disturbance perspective.

With it being 2025, it's sad overall that We Love Green are pioneers in the eco music festival space, in the very city that drew up the agreement ten years prior to limit warming to 1.5°C. ISO20121 is a strong start (especially since other third party verifications like B Corp are losing their reputation), but we need We Love Green to be the baseline for sustainability-centred events

High-key still missing Lula the blind dog, who was initially very vocal about the fact we were returning drenched at 2am until she could smell us 😭
