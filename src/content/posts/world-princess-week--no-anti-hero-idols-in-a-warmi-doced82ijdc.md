World Princess Week- no anti-hero idols in a warming era🌡️🌍

What was your favourite Disney Princess growing up? Mine was Ariel, with Pocohantas a close 2nd. 🧜‍♀️

This you may be able to answer, along with favourite singers and actors. But why as girls growing up, aren't we asked which leader (be it activist or politician) we support/admire? 💫

The people given the limelight today are barely connected with climate & the planet, & unless you watched lots of Davey A documentaries growing up🐢, the media we consume as children is frequently a more '"palatable" interpretation of the world that encourages the perception of nature as "other". Of the original fairytales, all Disney princess stories are stripped of deeper intersections with nature, and only Moana and Pocohantas, as portraying Indigenous women, show any depth of connection with the natural world 🏞️

Princesses, celebrities, the upper class. They're all idolised, and with this power can come with selling a brand, a name, to the rest of us muggins who want a bit of the cake. Yet as Patrick Grant writes in 'Less', any 'happiness' related to getting a product or an image is superficial, and spurs on addiction and envy.

I got this dress on depop, half price after it went viral last year as all the girlies who bought it new and wore it once were selling it on (as it was advertised VERY well and endorsed by influencers - the new breed of celebrity). Whilst its popularity helped its recognition of being desirable in my eyes, I love satin, pale blue, and a twirly circle skirt, and it has already been designated a special place in my wardrobe🩵. Where I christened it, the Cotswolds, is a desirable place, but Biddestone is rarely advertised as one of the key attractions, despite bunting, honey-coloured cottages, green spaces, white pigeon residents, and couple of manor houses. I think many of us would be happier not feeling the pressures of consumerism & just being able to do stuff we liked, like dressing up and going to an ad hoc village with boyfriend-made veggie burgers after work on a Friday. Wasps not included

These days, Mary Robinson, Nina Gualinga, Greta Thunberg, Shadi Sadr are my "princesses" 👸
