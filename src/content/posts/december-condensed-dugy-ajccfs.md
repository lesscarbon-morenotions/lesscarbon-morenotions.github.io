December condensed: ❄️☃️🎄

Christmas holiday season starts, as it has done for the last few years, with Corey's birthday and my round trip of seeing everyone in Dub 🇮🇪. Few things make me happier than seeing my friends and eating/drinking at independents 🫕🧉

Bath, Covent Garden and Kingston on Thames Christmas markets, going back up north and making a gingerbread house with the fam, and spending my Grannie's birthday weekend with her, before whisking the birthday boyf off to an array of London indie eats and drinks

New Year's watching fireworks for free (& in warmth) from my bedroom window, before moving house (again). Tbh I thought it would be a start of a good year, to shake off the hideous last but it's been a new kind of hard.

I've been lucky enough, since being with my boyfriend, to have experienced a wall of generosity, kindness, and of course nourishment (how had I survived for so long without saffron bastani, ghormeh sabzi, and tahchin??) from the Persian people I have come to love, know, and befriend.

In Persian, the most powerful and meaningful way to say "I love you" translates literally to, "I would sacrifice myself for you". It may seem a little drastic to native English language speakers, but that's the type of people Iranians are. Most of the death toll are people under 30, who have made the ultimate sacrifice in order for their loved ones and their country to be free. Strangers on the streets helping and uplifting each other even when those monsters break their bodies and attempt to break their souls. Too many parents have buried their children and then have been tortured to stay silent about their unimaginable loss these last few weeks.

All I want for a belated Christmas is my boyfriend to be able to go to his home, hug his mum, granny and cousins, taste all the incredible fruits from his grandpa's land, and do that as many times as he wants.

Azadi, آزادی
